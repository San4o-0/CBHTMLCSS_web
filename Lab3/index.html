Frequently asked questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to another plan at any time, and the new price applies from the next billing month.

What counts as a user?

A user is one person with their own login. Basic includes one user, Pro includes five, and Enterprise has no limit.

What is the difference in support?

Basic support answers by email on business days. Pro requests are handled first, and Enterprise teams get support 24/7.
